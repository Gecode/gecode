# Research report: parallel-search-worker-control

## Question

> Verify issue parallel-search-worker-control-007: Stress resize races and lifecycle transitions

## Summary

> Issue 007 should be treated as a verification-and-regression phase, not as a timing-only soak test. Its evidence must combine deterministic, fixed-seed lifecycle scenarios with repeated concurrent limit updates and sanitizer runs. The harness needs to exercise DFS and BAB while limits change during exploration and solution delivery, then cross lifecycle boundaries for stop, reset, restart, no-good extraction, constrain, PBS stop propagation, and destruction. Any defect found inside worker-control scope should become a focused regression; unrelated sanitizer findings should be recorded but not expanded into broad cleanup. Completion requires a command-level validation record for ThreadSanitizer, AddressSanitizer, and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer, plus high-iteration fixed-seed stress runs. The key correctness properties are absence of races, lifetime errors, undefined behavior, and deadlocks, while preserving completeness, BAB optimality, and eventual execution of parked owners.

## Findings

### The verification surface spans scheduler and lifecycle transitions

> The stress harness must vary worker limits during active search and across engine-wide transitions. Coverage that tests resizing only during ordinary exploration would miss explicit acceptance scope.

Evidence:
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > Add a deterministic stress harness and sanitizer validation matrix that repeatedly changes limits during exploration, solution delivery, stop, reset, restart, no-good extraction, constrain, and destruction.
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/brief.md` — > Wake parked workers for reset, termination, and other engine-wide lifecycle transitions.

### Deterministic regressions are required in addition to sanitizer stress

> Repeated concurrent runs are evidence for race freedom, but every discovered failure must also be reduced to a focused regression that does not depend only on favorable timing.

Evidence:
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > Failures are reproducible through focused regression tests rather than timing-only scripts.
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > Repeat the concurrency stress harness with fixed seeds and high iteration counts.

### All three sanitizer classes are acceptance requirements

> The validation matrix must include ThreadSanitizer for data races and ASan plus UBSan for lifetime and undefined-behavior failures; an ordinary threaded build alone is insufficient.

Evidence:
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > ThreadSanitizer reports no races across repeated concurrent resize scenarios.
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > ASan and UBSan report no lifetime or undefined-behavior failures.

### Progress properties must be checked, not inferred from clean sanitizer output

> The harness needs bounded completion checks for reset, termination, PBS stop propagation, destruction, and parked-owner reactivation. Sanitizers do not establish freedom from deadlock or false exhaustion.

Evidence:
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > Stress runs do not deadlock under reset, termination, PBS stop propagation, or destruction.
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/brief.md` — > A parked owner eventually executes when it holds the remaining search, so `next()` neither reports false exhaustion nor waits forever.

### Correctness remains semantic even though ordering may change

> Resize stress should compare solution sets and proven optima, not node counts or sequential solution order, because those observables are expressly unstable after resizing.

Evidence:
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/brief.md` — > Completeness and optimization correctness are required. Sequential search order, deterministic solution order, and stable node counts are not promised after resizing.
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/brief.md` — > DFS enumeration returns every expected solution exactly once across repeated shrink and growth sequences.

### The validation record must be reproducible and scoped

> Record exact configurations, commands, seeds, and iteration counts. Fix defects attributable to worker control, while keeping unrelated sanitizer cleanup outside the issue.

Evidence:
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > The validation record lists exact configurations and commands used.
- `.zd/parallel-search-worker-control/issues/007-stress-resize-races-and-lifecycle-transitions.md` — > Broad sanitizer cleanup outside search-worker control.

## Limitations

- The captured sources specify required scenarios and outcomes but do not identify existing sanitizer build directories, platform support, test names, or command-line conventions.
- The packet contains no implementation diff or test output, so this research result defines the verification boundary but does not itself satisfy any acceptance criterion.
- The sources do not set numeric timeout or iteration thresholds; the implementation and verification handoffs must choose and record values that are strong enough to expose races without making the suite impractical.

## Recommendations

- Build one focused deterministic test family covering DFS, BAB, RBS, and PBS, with fixed resize schedules and explicit bounded completion.
- Add a concurrent controller thread that cycles legal limits while the consumer calls next(), then repeat fixed seeds at high iteration counts.
- Exercise stop, reset, restart, no-good extraction, external BAB constrain, and destruction as separate focused cases so failures can be localized.
- Run the focused family in threaded release/debug configurations and under TSan, ASan, and UBSan; record exact configure, build, and test commands plus seeds and counts.
- When a failure appears, minimize it into a deterministic regression before applying an in-scope worker-control fix.
